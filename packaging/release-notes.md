APOST3Dview @VERSION@.

**Download** the file for your system below:

- **Windows** (64-bit): `APOST3Dview-@VERSION@-windows-x86_64.zip`
- **macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later): `APOST3Dview-@VERSION@-macos-universal.dmg`
- **Linux** (x86_64): `APOST3Dview-@VERSION@-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`

Each download holds an `examples` folder with five APOST-3D calculations to try: open a `.apost.json` in it, and its README says what to look at.

The builds are not signed by a paid certificate, so Windows and macOS ask once before the first start. What to click is in the [installation instructions](https://github.com/mgimferrer/APOST3Dview#installation).

Problems or questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/mgimferrer/APOST3Dview/issues).
