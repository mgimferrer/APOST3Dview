APOST3Dview @VERSION@.

**Download** the file for your computer below:

- **Windows** 10 or 11: `APOST3Dview-windows.zip`
- **macOS** 11 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel): `APOST3Dview-macos.dmg`
- **Linux** (x86_64): `APOST3Dview-linux.tar.gz`

Each download holds an `examples` folder with five APOST-3D calculations to try, and the user guide (`guide/index.html`, also online at https://mgimferrer.github.io/APOST3Dview/), whose five-minute tour uses the examples.

The first time you open it, Windows and macOS ask once whether to trust it (APOST3Dview is free, so it isn't signed with a paid certificate). What to click is in the [user guide](https://mgimferrer.github.io/APOST3Dview/install.html).

Problems or questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/mgimferrer/APOST3Dview/issues).
