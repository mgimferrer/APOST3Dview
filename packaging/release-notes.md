APOST3Dview @VERSION@.

**Download** the file for your computer below:

- **Windows** 10 or 11: `APOST3Dview-windows.zip`
- **macOS** 11 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel): `APOST3Dview-macos.dmg`
- **Linux** (x86_64): `APOST3Dview-linux.tar.gz`

Each download holds an `examples` folder with five APOST-3D calculations to try: open a `.apost.json` in it, and its README says what to look at.

The first time you open it, Windows and macOS ask once whether to trust it (APOST3Dview is free, so it isn't signed with a paid certificate). What to click is in the [installation instructions](https://github.com/mgimferrer/APOST3Dview#installation).

Problems or questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/mgimferrer/APOST3Dview/issues).
